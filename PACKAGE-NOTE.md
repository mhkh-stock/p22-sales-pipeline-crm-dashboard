# P22 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will build your sales pipeline dashboard and forecast next month's revenue

**What the client gets:** a sales pipeline / CRM dashboard for
their business — pipeline stages with conversion at every step
and the biggest leak named, win rate overall and per rep /
product / source, average deal size and sales cycle, the rep
leaderboard, why deals were lost (ranked by count and by money),
and a next-month forecast built deal by deal from expected close
dates, with a run-rate beside it so the forecast can be checked.
Delivered as a 2-page PDF report plus a live Excel workbook they
can re-run on next month's CRM export themselves.

## Tiers (Hashir's band)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | The 2-page PDF report on up to 100 deals: total pipeline, win rate, revenue won, the funnel with the biggest leak, the July-style next-month forecast, the 4 recommendations |
| Standard | $65 | 3 days | Everything in Basic + up to 500 deals, win rates by rep, product and source, the rep leaderboard, lost reasons ranked by money lost, average sales cycle, the live Excel workbook with formulas |
| Premium | $140 | 5 days | Everything in Standard + up to 2,000 deals, a forecast for the next 2 months with pipeline coverage, a fix-first plan for the biggest leak and the weakest rep, a reusable pipeline template for their own future CRM exports, 1 revision round |

**What's NOT included:** running their CRM or talking to their
customers (they run their own sales — quoted separately if they
need the pipeline process written); changing their prices or
discount rules (this service shows them where price objections
kill deals); a live dashboard that updates itself (this service
is a report + a workbook they re-run — a live dashboard is a
different gig).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads
their CRM export, not from order placement.
